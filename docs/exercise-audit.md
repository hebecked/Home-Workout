# Exercise text and illustration audit

Last updated: 2026-09-01

This is the canonical evidence checklist for all 51 bundled exercise definitions. Each source is an exercise-specific clinical, public-health, or accredited fitness-professional reference. Text and pose were reviewed separately against the linked reference and the generated all-exercise contact sheet. The review covers the German and English instructions, exercise identity, supports and floor contacts, direction of movement, and required equipment.

## Corrections made during the completed audit

- Assisted Pull-up now declares both a pull-up bar and a resistance band.
- Wall Sit and Hamstring Stretch now declare the wall or stable support shown in their instructions and illustration.
- Split Squat and Single-leg Glute Bridge now use a per-side repetition target.
- Side Plank explicitly uses the forearm and tells the user to change sides halfway through a timed set.
- Burpee now describes the standard push-up and jump sequence; Squat to Reach remains the easier low-impact alternative.
- Triceps Dip now specifies a low stable support and a pain-free shoulder range.

## Visual review method

The generated contact sheet was inspected once at phone-card scale and once at full asset scale. Moving exercises use same-scale overlaid positions with common hand, foot, hip, bar, or support anchors; they do not place separate figures side by side. Static holds and stretches use one pose unless a second overlay is essential to explain a joint movement. Every figure was checked for exactly two arms and two legs where visible, plausible floor and equipment contact, consistent body scale, and an anatomically readable silhouette.

The unit test `tests/unit/exercise-audit.test.ts` prevents an exercise from being added or removed without updating this checklist and requires every row to retain separate `Reviewed / Reviewed` status.
