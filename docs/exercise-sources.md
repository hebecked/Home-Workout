# Exercise illustration sources

The built-in illustrations are original local SVGs, not copied diagrams. All 33 now use a movement-specific start and finish pose. Their pose system and movement cues use the following references:

- **Glute Bridge:** supine start, bent knees and feet on the floor; hips lift while shoulders and feet stay grounded. Source: [American Council on Exercise — Glute Bridge](https://www.acefitness.org/resources/everyone/exercise-library/49/glute-bridge/).
- **Dead Bug:** supine position with hips and knees near 90 degrees; opposite arm and leg lower toward the floor before alternating sides. Source: [American Council on Exercise — Supine Dead Bug](https://www.acefitness.org/resources/everyone/exercise-library/147/supine-dead-bug/).
- **Lying Leg Raises:** supine position; straight legs lift together and lower under control toward, but not onto, the floor. Source: [Oxford Health NHS Foundation Trust — Core Strength (PDF)](https://oxfordhealth.nhs.uk/wp-content/uploads/sites/22/2023/10/Core-Strength.pdf).
- **Squats, lunges, and wall sits:** knees bend while the hips lower, a lunge uses a split stance, and a wall sit keeps the back at the wall. Source: [NHS — Knee exercises for runners](https://www.nhs.uk/live-well/exercise/knee-exercises-for-runners/).
- **Wide squat and Pull-up:** the NHS Strength and Flex exercise plan includes movement demonstrations for both. Source: [NHS — Strength and Flex exercise plan](https://www.nhs.uk/live-well/exercise/strength-and-flex-exercise-plan-how-to-videos/).
- **Pull-up and Assisted Pull-up:** standing beneath a bar, pulling upward without swinging, and resistance-band assistance were checked against [Cleveland Clinic — How To Do Pull-Ups](https://health.clevelandclinic.org/pull-ups) and [ACE — 4 Moves to Help You Master the Pull-up](https://www.acefitness.org/resources/pros/expert-articles/5565/4-moves-to-help-you-master-the-pull-up/).
- **Side Plank:** elbow beneath shoulder and a straight raised line through the body were checked against [Mayo Clinic — Core-strength exercises](https://www.mayoclinic.org/healthy-lifestyle/fitness/in-depth/core-strength/art-20546851) and [ACE — Side Plank with Straight Leg](https://www.acefitness.org/resources/everyone/exercise-library/101/side-plank-with-straight-leg/).
- **Pike Push-up:** the illustration uses the sourced inverted-V alignment from [ACE — Downward-facing Dog](https://www.acefitness.org/resources/everyone/exercise-library/18/downward-facing-dog/) and adds the clearly separated bent-elbow lowering phase described in the app.
- **Push-up:** the body remains a straight supported line while the arms lower and raise the upper body; hands and toes remain the floor contacts in the standard variation. Source: [ACE — Muscular Strength and Endurance Testing (PDF)](https://www.acefitness.org/groupfitnessresources/pdfs/GFI_Assessments.pdf).
- **Other bodyweight, band, core, and integrated poses:** exercise identity, equipment, target body area, and reference form were checked against the [ACE Exercise Library](https://www.acefitness.org/resources/everyone/exercise-library/) and its [no-equipment catalogue](https://www.acefitness.org/resources/everyone/exercise-library/equipment/no-equipment/).

The colored translucent figure shows the start or alternate position. The dark figure shows the finish or primary working position. The small arrow shows the primary direction of travel. Written instructions in the app reinforce lower-back control and deliberate movement.

The operational per-exercise review is tracked in [`exercise-audit.md`](exercise-audit.md). A general catalogue reference is not considered enough to close that audit: every row needs an exercise-specific source plus separate text and pose sign-off.
